# Claim scope

The construction fixes a positive sphere radius and supplied optimism parameter independently of dimension and uses the exact boundary from Lemma 7 of the FAST paper. The noiseless simplex requires only one constraint. Either δ or δ/5 can be used internally at ε=1/4 and δ=1/8.

Failure means violation of the near-optimal margin guarantee. The output vertex has positive margin. The proof concerns the unchanged boundary with a fixed-radius uniform sphere. Gaussian laws, dimension-growing radii, the empirical stopping refinement and dimension-dependent optimism remain outside the claim.
